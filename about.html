Foodie Lover

I absolutely love food, specially my home country's traditional cuisine. If I had to introduce you to my Portuguese favorites... well, it is incredibly hard to pinpoint my favorite dish but "Carne de Porco à Alentejana" is definitely one of them, after my favorite soup - "Caldo Verde" - as a starter. And a dessert that cannot be missed is "Bolo de Bolacha", which literally translates to cookie cake and it is the taste of my childhood. To finish you would have an Espresso with the classic, most famous and my all-time favorite pastry: the "Pastel de Nata".

The phenomenal Caldo Verde (Cabbage Soup).

The legendary Carne de Porco à Alentejana (Alentejo-style pork and clams).
